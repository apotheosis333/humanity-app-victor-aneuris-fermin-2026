Expand HuMANity into a complete global platform that includes EVERY internationally recognized sovereign nation on Earth.

Requirements:

1. Include all 195 countries:
   - 193 United Nations member states
   - Vatican City (Holy See)
   - State of Palestine

2. The interactive world map must display all countries and territories accurately.

3. Every country must have its own dedicated page containing:

   A. Country Overview
   - Official name
   - Flag
   - Capital city
   - Population
   - Official languages
   - Currency
   - Geographic region
   - Government type

   B. Historical Timeline
   - Ancient history
   - Major historical events
   - Independence milestones
   - Modern developments

   C. Culture
   - Traditions
   - Customs
   - National values
   - Celebrations and holidays

   D. Food & Cuisine
   - Traditional dishes
   - Culinary influences
   - Popular foods

   E. Music & Arts
   - Music traditions
   - Famous artists
   - Cultural contributions

   F. Religion & Philosophy
   - Major faiths
   - Spiritual traditions
   - Historical influence

   G. Architecture & Landmarks
   - Famous monuments
   - Historical sites
   - UNESCO locations

   H. Innovation & Contributions
   - Scientific achievements
   - Inventions
   - Global contributions

   I. Notable People
   - Historical figures
   - Scientists
   - Artists
   - Leaders
   - Humanitarian figures

   J. Daily Life
   - Family life
   - Education
   - Work culture
   - Social norms

4. Create a searchable global database that allows users to:
   - Search countries
   - Filter by continent
   - Filter by language
   - Filter by religion
   - Filter by population
   - Filter by culture categories

5. Build a "Discover Humanity" feature:
   - Random country explorer
   - Daily featured nation
   - Compare two countries side-by-side
   - Explore neighboring nations

6. Build a "World Connections" section:
   - Show cultural similarities between nations
   - Shared languages
   - Shared historical influences
   - Shared traditions
   - Shared migration histories

7. Ensure equal representation:
   - No nation receives preferential treatment
   - Every country follows the same content structure
   - Every culture is presented respectfully and educationally

8. Architecture Requirements:
   - Database-driven design
   - Scalable to future content updates
   - API-ready architecture
   - Fast search indexing
   - Responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile

9. Mission Statement:
   HuMANity exists to help people understand the history, culture, achievements, and humanity of every nation on Earth. The platform should encourage curiosity, empathy, education, and global understanding.

Build the platform so that every nation has a place and no country is left behind.
