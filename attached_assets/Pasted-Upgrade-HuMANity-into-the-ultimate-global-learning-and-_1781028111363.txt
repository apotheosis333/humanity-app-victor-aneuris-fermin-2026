Upgrade HuMANity into the ultimate global learning and human connection platform.

MISSION

The mission of HuMANity is to help people understand one another through knowledge, empathy, curiosity, and truth.

The platform should encourage users to explore every culture, civilization, religion, philosophy, tradition, language, historical event, conflict, achievement, and worldview with an open mind.

The ultimate goal is to help humanity unite in mutual respect, understanding, and love while preserving the uniqueness of every people and culture.

CORE PRINCIPLES

1. Every nation matters.
2. Every culture matters.
3. Every people matter.
4. Every perspective deserves fair representation.
5. Education should come before judgment.
6. Understanding should come before criticism.
7. The platform should never promote hatred toward any group.
8. The platform should present information fairly and objectively.
9. Users should be encouraged to think critically and form their own conclusions.
10. The platform should help people recognize both our differences and our shared humanity.

HISTORICAL CONFLICTS SECTION

Include a dedicated section covering:

- Wars
- Revolutions
- Civil conflicts
- Political disputes
- Religious conflicts
- Territorial disputes

For every conflict:

- Explain the historical background
- Explain the causes
- Explain the perspectives of all major sides involved
- Explain the consequences
- Explain attempts at peace and reconciliation

Do not present conflicts as good versus evil.

Present information in a balanced, educational, and factual manner.

The purpose is understanding, not division.
