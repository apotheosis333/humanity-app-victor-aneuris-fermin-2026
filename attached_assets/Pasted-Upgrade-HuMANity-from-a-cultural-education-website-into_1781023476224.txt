Upgrade HuMANity from a cultural education website into a premium global empathy platform designed to help humanity understand, respect, and connect with one another.

Core mission:
“Helping humanity see itself as one family without erasing what makes each culture unique.”

Build these major features:

1. Earth View Homepage
Create a cinematic homepage with a rotating Earth/globe visual. At first, show Earth without borders. When users interact, reveal countries and allow exploration. The emotional theme should be: before borders, politics, and conflict, we are one human family.

2. Human Stories
Add a section on every country page called “A Day In My Life.”
This should eventually contain first-person stories from ordinary citizens:
- Students
- Parents
- Workers
- Artists
- Elders
- Farmers
- Teachers
- Doctors
- Entrepreneurs

3. Walk In Their Shoes AI
Create a feature where users can choose a country, region, age, and occupation, then receive an immersive first-person day-in-the-life experience from that perspective. Make this educational, respectful, and empathy-focused.

4. World Dinner Table
Create a virtual global discussion feature where people from different nations answer the same universal questions:
- What does family mean to you?
- What makes you happy?
- What does peace mean to you?
- What do you hope for your children?
- What do you wish the world understood about your people?

5. Humanity Score
Create a personal learning dashboard that tracks:
- Countries explored
- Cultures learned
- Languages sampled
- Stories read
- Perspectives experienced
- Peace projects discovered

This is not a competition. It should feel like personal growth.

6. Global Music Explorer
Add music discovery to each country page:
- Traditional music
- Folk instruments
- Modern music
- Cultural soundscapes
- Famous artists

7. World Language Discovery
Each country page should teach simple phrases in major local languages:
- Hello
- Thank you
- Peace
- Friendship
- I love you
- Welcome

8. Humanity Timeline
Build an interactive timeline showing what different civilizations were doing during the same historical periods. Example: 1000 BCE should show developments in Egypt, China, Israel, India, Greece, Africa, and the Americas side by side.

9. Shared Humanity Map
Create a feature that highlights similarities between cultures:
- Family values
- Hospitality
- Respect for elders
- Community life
- Education
- Faith traditions
- Food customs
- Music patterns
- Migration history

10. Peace Projects
Add a section where users can discover real-world humanitarian projects:
- Clean water
- Education
- Medical aid
- Conflict resolution
- Food security
- Refugee support
- Environmental healing

11. Future of Humanity
Create a collaborative section focused on humanity’s biggest challenges:
- Poverty
- Disease
- Education
- Climate
- Energy
- AI
- Space exploration
- War and peace

12. Humanity Pledge
Add an optional pledge users can sign:
“I will seek understanding before judgment.
I will value the dignity of every human being.
I will remain curious about cultures different from my own.
I recognize that humanity is stronger when we learn from one another.”

Design style:
Make the platform feel premium, cinematic, peaceful, and futuristic. Use the existing HuMANity color palette:
- Midnight Blue: #0F172A
- Royal Blue: #2563EB
- Warm Gold: #FBBF24
- Pure White: #FFFFFF
- Soft Light Background: #F8FAFC
- Charcoal Text: #1F2937

Brand styling:
- Write the name exactly as “HuMANity”
- Make “MAN” Warm Gold
- Make the rest Royal Blue or White depending on background
- Use gold only for meaningful highlights, selected countries, important icons, and special moments

User experience:
The app should feel like a digital United Nations for ordinary people. It should not feel like a generic encyclopedia or social media app. It should feel inspiring, emotionally intelligent, respectful, educational, and world-class.

Technical requirements:
- Mobile-responsive
- Scalable database structure
- Country pages generated from structured data
- Clean component architecture
- Searchable countries
- Filter by continent, language, culture, religion, population, and region
- Future-ready for user accounts, AI features, translations, and community stories

Build this as a polished MVP first, using placeholder data where needed, but structure the code so real content, APIs, and AI integrations can be added later.
